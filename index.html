About Arithmos

The Game

Arithmos is a strategy game of numbers and nerve. Five houses compete across three auction rounds, bidding for mathematicians, then formulas and equations, and finally for the identity that will define their house.

The Kingdom

Beneath a moonlit castle, one kingdom is split between five rival houses. Every house wants the throne of reason, and only the sharpest strategy will win it.

Strategy & Mathematics

Every bid is a calculation. Know the value of what you buy, read the other houses, and decide when to hold your coin.
